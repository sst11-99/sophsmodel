The approved design includes taped model cards, individual torn cream tape and shadows, separate-photo profiles, Female/Male/AI divisions, and recoverable applications with verified receipts. Older version branches are historical backups, not separate live websites.

25 September 2026: phone/tablet/desktop layout checks passed at 320×568, 360×800, 412×915, 800×360, 768×1024, 1024×768 and 1440×900. These are browser viewport checks, not physical Android certification. Large JPEG/PNG camera images are resized on-device before upload; ordinary smaller images are retained. The application follows dynamic viewport resizing, including Chrome's supported keyboard resize mode. No separate Android site or app is needed.
